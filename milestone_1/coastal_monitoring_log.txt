The coastal monitoring team began its morning survey shortly after sunrise.

A section of the eastern boardwalk was closed after inspectors noticed loose wooden panels.

The weather station recorded strong winds during the first hour of the survey.

A maintenance crew secured the damaged section and placed temporary warning barriers around the area.

The observation tower remained open and no visitor injuries were reported.

By afternoon, wind conditions improved and the survey team completed the remaining shoreline checks.

A follow-up structural inspection of the eastern boardwalk is scheduled for Thursday.
